      Neighbourhoods are Statistics Canada dissemination areas (DAs), each home to roughly 400 to 700 people. Each is named after the two streets with the most length inside it. Values marked n/a were suppressed by Statistics Canada to protect privacy.

      Incomes are for the 2020 calendar year, when pandemic benefits raised some low incomes. City figures are Statistics Canada's own figures for the City of Guelph. Most measures past population and age come from the 25% long-form sample, so small neighbourhoods can swing a lot. Work figures describe May 2021, during the pandemic.
